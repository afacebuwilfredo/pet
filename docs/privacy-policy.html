Life Buddy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

Life Buddy is a virtual pet app. This policy explains what information the app uses. The short version: Life Buddy does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

Who we are

Life Buddy is published as an independent Android app (package com.littlebuddy.fred). You can contact the developer through the GitHub issues page for this project.

Information the app does not collect

Life Buddy does not ask for an account and does not collect:

  • Name, email, phone number, or other contact details
  • Location, photos, contacts, or microphone audio
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers or analytics events
  • Crash reports or usage statistics sent to a server

Information that stays on your device

Pet name, mood, coins, XP, and care stats exist only inside the app on your device for the current session. In version 0.1.1 this progress is not uploaded to our servers. Closing the app may reset progress because it is not yet saved to a cloud account.

Internet access

The app does not need an account to play. It does not send pet data to Life Buddy servers. Google Play may still use its own services when you install or update the app. Those services are covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Children

Life Buddy is a casual virtual-pet experience. Version 0.1.1 is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a later version of the app, contact us and we will delete it.

Third parties

We do not sell personal information. We do not share user data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies.

Data deletion and access

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no cloud profile to download or delete. Uninstalling the app removes the on-device session.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, cloud saves, ads, or analytics, this policy will be updated before those features go live, and the Play Store Data safety form will be updated to match.

Contact

Questions about this policy: github.com/afacebuwilfredo/pet/issues
