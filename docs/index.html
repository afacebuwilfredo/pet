Full description

Life Buddy is a cozy virtual pet for Android. Meet Buddy, a friendly puppy who needs your care. Feed him when he is hungry, play when he wants to have fun, and let him nap when his energy is low. Watch hunger, happiness, energy, and health change as you look after him. Earn XP and coins as you care for Buddy. Level up your pet and keep him happy with simple, calming interactions. Version 0.1.1 includes: • Feed, Play, and Sleep actions • Hunger, Happiness, Energy, and Health bars • XP, levels, and coins • A lightweight companion you can open anytime No account is required. Life Buddy does not collect personal information in this version. Have fun taking care of your Life Buddy.

Data safety

Play Console > App content > Data safety. Answer as follows for v0.1.1:

  • Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? No
  • Is all user data encrypted in transit? Skip / not applicable. The app does not collect user data.
  • Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? Skip / not applicable. No cloud account or stored personal data.
  • Independent security review: No
  • Data collected: None
  • Data shared: None
  • Sold / used for ads / used for tracking: No

Observed in the current app code: pet stats live in device memory only. There is no login, analytics, ads, or backend.

Target audience

Play Console > App content > Target audience and content.

  • Target age groups: 13–15, 16–17, and 18 and over
  • Appeals to children: No
  • Designed for Families: No, do not enroll for v0.1.1
  • News app: No
  • COVID-19 contact tracing / status: No

Choose 13+ even though the art is cute. Selecting under-13 puts the app in Google’s Families rules and COPPA-style requirements. Version 0.1.1 is a simple care toy, not a children’s social product.
